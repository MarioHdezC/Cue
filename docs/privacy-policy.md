# Privacy Policy for Cue

Last updated: February 20, 2026

Cue is a macOS menu bar app for daily task scheduling with timed reminders, developed by Mario Hernández Corral.

**Data Collection**
Cue does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All tasks and settings are stored locally on your device.

**Third-Party Services**
Cue does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

**Local Notifications**
Cue uses local notifications to send task reminders. These notifications are processed entirely on your device and no data is sent to external servers.

**Changes to This Policy**
If this policy is updated, changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
